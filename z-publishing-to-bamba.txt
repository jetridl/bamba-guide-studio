Publishing to Bamba

On Hetzner: edit, raise the authored version, approve, then Publish.
Studio saves persistent exports and pushes automatically to jetridl/bamba-content.
Failed pushes retry every 60 seconds; wait for Synced in Studio.

In bamba: ./tool/flutter run or ./tool/flutter build ipa
The command uses FVM and fetches, validates and registers latest content first.
No app content lock or manual sync is needed. Raw fvm flutter builds bypass it.
