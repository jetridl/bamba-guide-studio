TEXT VERIFICATION
Rossetti: Sing-Song: A Nursery Rhyme Book, Macmillan, 1893, via Project Gutenberg. The catalogue's wording, punctuation, contraction thro’, and two stanzas agree with this edition. Other anthologies expand the contraction or add commas; those variants were not substituted.
https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/76703/pg76703-images.html

Tennyson: Representative Poetry Online, University of Toronto Libraries, text of The Eagle citing the 1851 and 1891 editions. Wording and punctuation agree with the catalogue, including Ring'd. The catalogue's two tercets are retained.
https://rpo.library.utoronto.ca/content/eagle

Dickinson: The public-domain 1891 printing in Poems, Second Series was checked through Project Gutenberg, and the manuscript-based text was checked in Dartmouth's White Heat project, under “Hope” is the thing with feathers. The catalogue uses the manuscript-based wording, capitalisation and punctuation pattern, with hyphen-minus characters for dashes, conventional twelve-line verse layout and three stanzas. Dartmouth also shows manuscript line wrapping; it is not a different poem.
https://www.gutenberg.org/files/12242/12242-h/12242-h.htm
https://journeys.dartmouth.edu/whiteheat/september-10-16-1862-poems-on-birds/

Edition distinction: the 1891 printing regularises Dickinson's dashes and capitalisation. The approved Bamba transcription was preserved exactly; it has not been claimed to be a facsimile of the 1891 typography. The Journey's notes discuss the text actually bundled in Bamba.
