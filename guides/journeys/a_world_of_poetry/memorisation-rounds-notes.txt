HISTORICAL VERSION-3 RECORD: The five newer guides have since advanced to version 4, with text between practice groups and opening recaps of the preceding one or two days. See session-structure-notes.txt for the current structure and validation. Frost and Whitman remain at version 3.

All seven canonical guides have been advanced from version 2 to version 3.
Every one of their 31 learning days now practises the day's new lines in this order:

1. Last Word — last_word_game / lastWord
2. First Word — first_word_game / firstWord
3. First and Last Word — first_last_word_game / firstAndLast
4. Random Words — hidden_word_game / hiddenWord
5. First Letter — first_letter_game / firstLetter

Each round covers every new chunk once before moving to the next round type. Each exercise page refers explicitly to one existing chunk of at most two lines; the app does not have to infer its passage from the session. The sequence provides 335 daily exercise pages across the seven guides.

CHANGES AND PRESERVATION

The five recently created reading guides did not contain interactive game pages. They now include all five rounds after their existing reading and recall passage, before reflection and closing.

Frost and Whitman had four main exercise groups in a different order, and neither had a complete separate five-round sequence. Their existing daily game pages were reordered, and First and Last Word pages were added. Twenty-four Whitman pages labelled First-Word or First-Letter had mismatching saved exercise types; those types and roundType values now agree with the existing labels and instructions.

In total, 239 new exercise pages were added. All original page IDs are retained, including the reordered and corrected exercises. Newly added pages have stable session-prefixed IDs. All original poem, guide, stanza, line, chunk and session IDs remain unchanged.

Existing prior-day recap exercises are preserved at the beginning of their days. They are separate from the five rounds on the day's new lines. All original non-game pages retain their wording and relative order. Poem text, artwork, alt text, editorial notes, session metadata, cover briefs and approved/exported guide statuses are preserved. The Journey still references the same seven poem IDs and needs no version change.
